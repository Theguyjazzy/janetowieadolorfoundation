# PRODUCT.md — Janet Owieadolor Foundation (JOF)

## 1. Commission & Scope Boundary
- **Client / Organization:** Janet Owieadolor Foundation (`JOF`), a registered Nigerian non-profit organization headquartered at **22 Eromosele Street, Off College Road, Ekewan Road, Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria** (`+234 803 333 1232`, WhatsApp `+234 703 324 4322`, `info@janetowieadolorfoundation.com`).
- **Product Context (Real-World Work):** Uplifting indigent women, widows, single mothers, and orphans across Edo State (including **Abumwenre Community**, reaching **500+ widows** in single outreaches) and Nigeria (Enugu, Ogun, Anambra, Lagos) over **8+ years** through four core relief tracks: *Super Woman Relief Outreach* (food essentials), *Medical Check-ups & Optical Care*, *Agricultural & Livelihood Support* (farm tools and ₦50,000–₦75,000 micro-grants), and *Family Support & Child Scholarships* alongside pro-bono legal advocacy. Governed by Founder **Mama Janet Owieadolor**, Chairman **Dr. Francis Owieadolor**, six additional **Board of Trustees** members, and **Joy Oboite** (Editor & Programme Coordinator), collaborating with *Glorious Widows Fellowship* and *Widows Help Rendezvous*.
- **Website Scope:** Redesign the public multi-route digital experience (`Home`, `Stories` + individual story reader, `Founders & Trustees`, `Get Involved`, `Donate`) inside `sites/janet-owieadolor-foundation/`, preserving all existing functional routes, Convex CMS/form endpoints (`https://dazzling-cheetah-893.eu-west-1.convex.cloud`), WhatsApp pledge dispatch, and Paystack "Coming Soon" guard while completely replacing the generic visual template.

## 2. Three Guiding User Personas (Product Design Seed)

### Persona 1 — Dr. Osasere Igbinovia, 44 (Primary: Diaspora & Individual Donor)
- **Context:** Nigerian-born Senior Pharmacist in London with deep ties to Benin City; browses on MacBook Pro or iPhone from family WhatsApp referrals.
- **Jobs-to-Be-Done:** Direct ₦50,000–₦250,000 in charitable giving to a verifiable grassroots organization in Edo State where funds clearly reach households without administrative bloat.
- **Mental Model & Fluency:** Immediately distrusts generic "charity-template" aesthetics, stock photography, or helplessness clichés; looks for Benin City physical addresses, named Board Trustees, and concrete Naira-to-outcome figures.
- **Frustrations & Skepticism:** Unverifiable impact claims, slow preloader screens, pushy donation popups, or imagery that strips widows of dignity.
- **Decision Trigger:** Seeing Mama Janet's portrait alongside documented Abumwenre/Benin field photography, transparent governance, and an immediate ₦-tier WhatsApp pledge pathway.

### Persona 2 — Toluwani Adeyemi, 36 (Secondary: Corporate CSR & Institutional Partner)
- **Context:** Head of Sustainability & CSR at a Nigerian agribusiness/FMCG company reviewing community partners on a desktop monitor during annual CSR allocation.
- **Jobs-to-Be-Done:** Rapidly validate NGO legitimacy (8+ years track record, Board of Trustees, audited governance), programme taxonomy, and submit a partnership inquiry.
- **Mental Model & Fluency:** Reads credibility through structural rigor, governance clarity, and programme specificity rather than emotional slogans.
- **Frustrations & Skepticism:** Cluttered icon grids, missing leadership credentials, broken or unacknowledged inquiry forms.
- **Decision Trigger:** Clear Board of Trustees roster, 98.4% micro-grant independence metric, and frictionless CSR partnership submission (`partners:submit`).

### Persona 3 — Sister Eunice Okafor, 51 (High-Leverage: Community Advocate & Volunteer)
- **Context:** Parish Women's Guild Coordinator in Edo/Delta browsing on an Android phone over mobile data to refer a widowed neighbor or register as a volunteer.
- **Jobs-to-Be-Done:** Confirm eligibility/coverage (pro-bono land rights legal aid, medical screening, emergency food relief, child tuition), verify real beneficiary stories, and reach JOF via WhatsApp or phone immediately.
- **Mental Model & Fluency:** Relational, practical, action-first; needs fast page loads, crisp mobile typography, and human contact access.
- **Frustrations & Skepticism:** Buried phone numbers, heavy unoptimized pages, forms with unclear feedback.
- **Decision Trigger:** Authentic stories of named Nigerian women (`Adaeze O.`, `Ngozi A.`, `Chiamaka E.`, `Blessing U.`) and always-accessible direct WhatsApp / Support Line contact.

### Unified Subconscious Mental Model & Subtle Conversion Architecture
- **Subconscious Mental Model:** *Sovereign Warmth & Proven Stewardship* — Within 2 seconds, the visitor feels cultural dignity (women crowned in vibrant cloth and agency, never pity) grounded in institutional permanence (Benin City HQ, named Trustees, 8+ years of real field records).
- **Subtle Conversion Touchpoints:**
  1. Hero primary CTA (`Donate to an Outreach`) + direct programme exploration link.
  2. Interactive **Naira Impact Allocator** (`₦5,000` → `₦100,000`) inside the Field Chronicle section linking directly to pre-selected `/donate` tiers.
  3. Direct **WhatsApp & Support Line** rail in Contact and Donate views—zero coercive countdowns or popups.

## 3. Commissioned Temporary Creative Director
- **Director Identity:** **Ivie Osaigbovo — Architectural Textile & Print Monograph Director** (conceived independently from museum monograph publishing, Benin bronze-relief proportioning, and ceremonial *aso-oke / akwete* woven band geometry).
- **Core Thesis:** *"On this page, Benin City documentary field prints and ceremonial cloth proportions become an uneven architectural broadsheet."*
- **Visual & Structural Commitments:**
  - Replace all NGO card-grid clichés, glassmorphic navbars, pill eyebrow badges, and dark navy+gold gradients with quiet **Chalk Limestone (`#F4F2EC`)**, **Weathered Sand (`#EAE6DC`)**, **Bronze-Obsidian (`#141210`)**, and **Edo Coral-Crimson (`#9F2B1D`)** punctuated sparingly by **Ochre Brass (`#B5942E`)** from Mama Janet's crest.
  - Pair **Instrument Serif** (authoritative, quiet editorial headlines) with **Hanken Grotesk** (tabular-numeral precision and accessible body copy).
  - Compose every section with a distinct spatial topology: uneven gallery-wall hero prints, asymmetric diptych, dark bronze-obsidian interactive chronicle & Naira ledger, living monograph programme index, staggered broadsheet story dossier, interactive portrait pullquote stage, and sticky-uncover monumental wordmark footer.
- **Specialist Skill Decisions:**
  - `algorithmic-art`: **NO** — real documentary photographs of Mama Janet and community outreaches carry unmatched truth.
  - `MengTo/Skills` WebGL/Shaders: **NO** — pure editorial photography + GSAP/Lenis choreography avoids artificial tech ornament.
  - `Director Screen/Section Transitions`: **YES** — a crisp `420ms` Woven Veil route wipe in `#141210` between sub-pages, plus shutter clip-path image discloses on the hero and programme plate switcher.
